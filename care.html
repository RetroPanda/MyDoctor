Gender-Affirming Care

My Doctor Family Practice provides gender-affirming healthcare (GAHC) to individuals across South Africa. Consultations are available virtually through mobile/laptop device or in-rooms at our practice in Strandfontein.

Practice address:
65A Dennegeur Avenue,
Strandfontein, Cape Town

How to Book

First Consultation:

For first time patients to My Doctor Family Practice ready to start Hormone replacement therapy.

Select BOOK NOW on our website and choose your video or in-rooms GENDER AFFIRMING CARE - FIRST CONSULTATION

Alternatively, contact the practice and provide the following information:

  • Full name/s and surname (as per South African ID/passport)
  • Gender listed on South African ID/passport
  • Preferred name
  • Preferred gender identity (e.g. trans man, trans woman, non-binary)
  • South African ID number/Passport number
  • Cellphone number
  • Email address
  • WITS RHI / Ivan Toms folder number (if applicable)
  • Care requested: Virtual or In-rooms consultation
  • Requested consultation date and time

Follow-up Consultations:

For patients already receiving gender affirming care from My Doctor Family Practice.

Select Book now on our website and choose from our list of In-rooms and video consultations, complete your details and confirm booking.

GAHC Consultation Options

1st Consultation – In Rooms

For patients ready to start Hormone replacement therapy, prior to your consultation, download the applicable MyDr Gender Affirming Healthcare (GAHC) patient guide below:

Consultation Includes:

  • 30 minute In-rooms consultation at 65a Dennegeur Avenue, Strandfontein
  • Comprehensive medical history, assessment, and physical examination
  • Review and discuss the My Doctor Family Practice Gender-Affirming Healthcare (GAHC) Patient Guide to help you understand the treatment process, options and what to expect.
  • Blood tests taken in rooms, which may include:
    • STI screening
    • Glucose
    • Renal and liver function
    • Pregnancy Test (where applicable)
    • Testosterone or oestrogen levels (where applicable)
  • (Laboratory costs discussed during the consultation)
  • Referral recommendations and documents where appropriate (e.g. psychology or surgical services)
  • Gender marker change forms provided (where applicable)

1st Consultation – Video Consultation

For patients ready to start Hormone replacement therapy, prior to your consultation., download the applicable MyDr Gender Affirming Healthcare (GAHC) Patient Guide below:

Consultation Includes:

  • 30 minute Video consultation through your mobile or laptop device
  • Medical history and clinical assessment
  • Review and discuss the My Doctor Family Practice Gender-Affirming Healthcare (GAHC) Patient Guide to help you understand the treatment process, options and what to expect.
  • Pathology request form for blood tests at a walk-in laboratory
  • Referral recommendations and documents where appropriate (e.g. psychology or surgical services)
  • Information and consent forms to be completed and emailed to infomydr@gmail.com.
  • Gender marker change forms provided (where applicable)

Follow-Up and Ongoing Care

  • For existing gender-affirming hormone therapy patients requiring monitoring, blood-result review or prescription management.
  • Video or In-rooms consultation
  • Prescriptions are issued based on results and agreed treatment plans.
  • Download the MyDr Gender affirming Healthcare (GAC) Patient Guide below for more information.

Important Information

  • My Doctor Family Practice is a private practice and not a free clinic
  • Patients are responsible for all consultation, laboratory, and medication costs
  • Prescriptions will not be issued without the required blood test results
  • Medications must be obtained from:
    • Compounding pharmacies or
    • Local pharmacies, where applicable
  • (at the patient’s own cost)
