Consultations

Fees applicable from 1st March 2026; pricing subject to change.

In-Person Consultations

Fee: R450
Duration: Up to 30 minutes
Includes:

  • Comprehensive medical assessment
  • Diagnosis and management of acute and chronic conditions
  • Basic medication dispensed on site (where clinically appropriate)
  • Access to in-room procedures when indicated

Extended consultation time allows for thorough assessment and personalised care.

Virtual Consultations

Fee: R335
Duration: Up to 15 minutes
Available to patients anywhere in South Africa

Suitable for:

  • Follow-up consultations
  • Minor acute illnesses
  • Chronic condition reviews
  • Results discussions
  • Medical advice and electronic prescriptions (where appropriate)

Home Visit Consultations

R900 within 15 km of the practice; R1000 for distances beyond 15 km
Duration: Up to 60 minutes
Service area: Cape Town
Contact reception to book

Ideal for:

  • Elderly or immobile patients
  • Acute illness requiring in-home assessment
  • Post-hospital or post-procedure follow-ups
  • Patients requiring extended consultation time in a home setting

Home visit consultations must be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance. Our practice does not offer same-day bookings for home visits.
